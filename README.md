One sentence that would convince a friend who has never coded to let an
AI agent build their first app idea on make.fun.

> That app idea you've been carrying in your head — just tell it to an AI agent on make.fun and watch it become an app you can tap and show your friends, no code, nothing to install, and if it's not what you pictured, just walk away.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only, no network) writes the sentence to
`/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`). Deterministic:
two runs produce byte-identical output (231 characters, 233 bytes,
SHA-256 `454aefa03e95b341084d349ae840a258cb0a73a9955388c31f59e8a5960da3ca`).
Revised once in-session (24 Nov 2026): the opening now leads with the
friend's own idea instead of an instruction; see WORKING-NOTES.md.
